# AI Builder Cup 2026

**Research date:** 2026-09-23  
**Primary official site:** [aibuildercup.com](https://aibuildercup.com/)  
**Organizer:** Hack2skill  
**Sponsor/technical partner named by the site:** Google Cloud  
**Region:** JAPAC

## Theme and premise

The event invites builders to start with a real-world problem, build a solution on Google Cloud, and demonstrate how a local solution can scale globally. The current challenge areas are:

1. **BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences** — banking, financial services, insurance, fraud/risk, process automation, customer interaction, and financial experiences.
2. **Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences** — discovery, personalization, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insights, and fraud.
3. **Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency** — efficiency, quality, reliability, supply/demand, sustainability, and operational-data insights.
4. **Media, Content & Digital Experiences** — content creation, discovery, personalization, understanding, and digital-content experiences.
5. **Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity** — knowledge access, collaboration, decisions, productivity, and workflow execution.
6. **Sustainability & Social Impact** — resource efficiency, accessibility, resilience, community support, and sustainable decision-making.

Each team may submit one solution under one theme/problem statement.

## Who can participate

- Working professionals, entrepreneurs, and startups.
- Participants must be **21+** and based in **JAPAC**.
- Students are not eligible. A team containing a student may be disqualified, including after shortlisting.
- Teams contain **2–4 members** during the build phase.
- Solo registration is allowed, but solo participation is not; the registrant must later join or form a team.
- Cross-country JAPAC teams are allowed.
- A participant may not register with multiple teams.

**Date discrepancy:** the FAQ and Terms state that the registration/roster-change window closes **4 Oct 2026**, while the overview timeline states team formation through **11 Oct 2026**. Confirm with the organizer before relying on either date.

## Journey and finale

Shortlisted teams are invited to demo live in Singapore before Google Cloud and industry experts. Only **two members per shortlisted team** may attend, regardless of build-phase team size. The site says finalist travel and stay are sponsored; the Terms/FAQ clarify the scope and conditions in [RULES.md](./RULES.md).
