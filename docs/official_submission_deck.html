Brief about the idea
"A compass, not a tutor — every answer anchored to the video with proof, in your language, and boundary-honest."
Core Concept & Mission
Transforming Passive Long-Form Video Into Verifiable, Interactive Knowledge

Millions of developers and students learn via YouTube lectures, coding tutorials, and technical talks. However, self-directed learners face massive friction points: scrubbing fatigue (hunting for a 10-second snippet), chatbot hallucination (AI answering from open-web training data instead of video ground truth), and linguistic isolation (non-native speakers struggling with dense English jargon).

ContextBridge AI solves this by introducing a bounded multimodal agent ladder powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash, providing sub-second seeking, verifiable EvidenceBadges, and full bilingual parity.

1. Anchored (Sole Truth)

The chosen video is the sole ground truth. Answers must originate from verified transcript lines or on-screen visual keyframes.

  • Exact character-match quote gate
  • Zero invented citations or facts
2. Visible Proof (1-Click)

Trust visible in seconds. Every answer displays an EvidenceBadge with confidence %, timestamp range, and quoted speaker words.

  • 1-Click seeks YouTube iframe player
  • Auto-scrolls transcript feed in lockstep
3. Boundary Honesty

When a question is NOT covered in the video, the agent admits absence with 0.00 confidence rather than guessing.

  • Honest refusal disclaimer
  • 3 Proactive next explore topics

• Integrity Gates: Exact quote character verification & confidence scorer

• Resilience: 9 failure-path drills, circuit breakers & quota fallbacks

• Streaming: Server-Sent Events (SSE) for low-latency delivery

Foundation AI Layer
Google Gemini 2.5 Flash

• Foundation Model: gemini-2.5-flash via Google GenAI SDK

• Multimodal Vision: Deep inspection of video frames & slides

• Determinism: Zero-temperature parameters to eliminate hallucination

• Grounding: Google Search fallback tool for external validation

• Bilingual Engine: Native Devanagari Hindi & English reasoning
