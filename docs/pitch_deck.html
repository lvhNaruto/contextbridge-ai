The Challenge 02 / 10

The Self-Learner's Dilemma

Billions of hours of educational video are published every year, but learners abandon them due to four structural friction points.

⏳ The Scrubbing Fatigue
Learners waste 15+ minutes scrubbing back and forth across 45-minute videos just to find the 10 seconds where a specific syntax, formula, or concept was explained.
🤖 The Chatbot Hallucination Trap
Standard chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude) don't have access to the exact video runtime. When asked, they invent plausible-sounding code, fake explanations, and quote words the instructor never spoke.
🗣️ The Linguistic Barrier
High-value technical content is overwhelmingly in English. Non-native speakers (e.g. Hindi, Hinglish) struggle with dense video jargon without native language bridges.
🌫️ The Unclear Boundary
Existing video summarizers fail to distinguish between what the creator actually claimed and general internet knowledge, eroding learner trust.

Core Philosophy 03 / 10

The Three Pillars of The Compass

ContextBridge is engineered with non-negotiable architectural gates that guarantee trust.

⚓
P1 · Anchored
The video is the sole ground truth. Video answers come strictly from the creator's spoken transcript, verified by the anti-fabrication gate. Quotes must match character-by-character.
🔍
P2 · Proof
Trust must be visible in 10 seconds. Every answer shows its origin badge. One click on an evidence chip seeks the video player to the exact second (~1s) while auto-scrolling the transcript.
🧭
P3 · Boundary Honesty
The boundary is a feature, not a bug. When curiosity steps outside the video, ContextBridge never blurs the lines. It frames external research in a distinct Boundary Card with verified Google Search sources.

Technical Architecture 06 / 10

The Anti-Fabrication Agent Gate

A 3-tool bounded state machine with mathematical limits on LLM rounds and zero hallucination escape routes.

// THE 3-TOOL BOUNDED LADDER
1. OBSERVE: retrieve_video_context(envelope, question)
├─ Lexical token matching
└─ Bounded Devanagari fallback window

2. EVALUATE: validate_answer_draft(draft, envelope)
├─ Check startSeconds & endSeconds ≤ video_duration
├─ quote_matches_transcript(verbatim substring check)
└─ Exactly 1 retry on gate failure → honest not_found

3. SELECT: gemini_web_research(query)
└─ Google Search Grounding (external boundary only)
Strict Budget: ≤ 2 LLM Rounds
The agent is deterministic and bounded. No runaway loops, no multi-agent chatter. Guaranteed fast response under 5 seconds.
Stateless Cloud Run Design
Client owns history (last ≤ 6 turns). Server maintains zero session state in memory or database, allowing infinite horizontal scaling.
